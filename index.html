Frequently asked, quietly

Is this a real product?+
No. Brown Eye Auth is a fictional company built for a joke about biometric security marketing. Nothing here is collected, stored, or real.
What if I gain or lose weight?+
Our matching model tolerates normal biological variance. Significant transformations may require re-enrollment and, frankly, a check-in.
What about identical twins?+
Even identical twins register distinct patterns. We have not independently verified this. We are fairly confident.
Is this covered by my benefits plan?+
Please direct that question to your benefits team. We'd prefer you not ask us again.

Please, God, no. This is for logging into Salesforce.
